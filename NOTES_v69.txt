KaizenCraft v69 foundation pass

Included in this zip:
- first-person body/camera offset rework aimed at showing lower body from eye level instead of inside the chest
- held item/block viewmodel in first person
- textured dropped items/blocks instead of flat solid colors when preview textures exist
- 24-minute full day cycle with 24-hour clock display backend and much higher configurable time speed cap
- smoother hour-based sunrise/sunset lighting transitions
- larger sun/moon and moving clouds that drift across the world instead of sticking to the player
- procedural sky material setup for a more proper sky backdrop
- sheep hitbox body and custom-model swap path kept
- configurable food/fuel/tool/item metadata in BlockRegistry
- raw mutton and cooked mutton item definitions, furnace/pickaxe item/block definitions, and new textures for them
- stronger chunk loading budgets / extra loaded margin aimed at reducing terrain holes
- safer water surface mesh offset and opaque water shader to reduce seam artifacts

Not fully complete yet:
- furnace UI / full smelting station workflow is not fully implemented in this pass
- full block rendering rewrite was not completed; this pass tightens the current system instead of replacing it
